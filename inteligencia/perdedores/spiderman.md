# Spider-Man

Spider-Man is a fictional superhero that appears in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. He was created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko. The character first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962).

## Enmigos

- **Green Goblin** — Norman Osborn, a wealthy industrialist and the father of Peter Parker's best friend, Harry. He becomes the Green Goblin after taking a serum that enhances his physical abilities but also drives him insane.

- **Doctor Octopus** — Otto Octavius, a brilliant scientist who becomes Doctor Octopus after an accident fuses mechanical tentacles to his body. He is one of Spider-Man's most formidable

## Peliculas
- **Spider-Man: Homecoming** (2017) — Directed by Jon Watts, this film follows Peter Parker as he navigates high school life while also embracing his role as Spider-Man.
- **Spider-Man: Far From Home** (2019) — Directed by Jon Watts, this film sees Peter Parker dealing with the aftermath of the events of Avengers: Endgame while facing a new threat in the form of Mysterio.
- **Spider-Man: No Way Home** (2021) — Directed by Jon Watts, this film explores the multiverse as Peter Parker seeks help from Doctor Strange to erase the knowledge of his secret identity from the world, leading to encounters with villains from other universes.
- **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** (2018) — Directed by Bob Persichetti, Peter Ramsey, and Rodney Rothman, this animated film introduces the concept of the Spider-Verse, where multiple versions of Spider-Man from different dimensions come together to stop a threat that could destroy all realities.
- **Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse** (2023) — Directed by Joaquim Dos Santos, Kemp Powers, and Justin K. Thompson, this animated sequel continues the story of Miles Morales as he navigates the challenges of being Spider-Man while encountering other Spider-People from different dimensions.

## conocidos de spider-man

- **Mary Jane Watson** — Peter Parker's love interest and a key character in his personal life.
- **Aunt May** — Peter Parker's aunt and a maternal figure in his life, often providing guidance and support.
- **J. Jonah Jameson** — The editor-in-chief of the Daily Bugle, a newspaper that frequently criticizes Spider-Man and portrays him as a menace to society.
