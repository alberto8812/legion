# Spider-Man

Spider-Man is a fictional superhero that appears in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. He was created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko. The character first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962).

## Enmigos

- **Green Goblin** — Norman Osborn, a wealthy industrialist and the father of Peter Parker's best friend, Harry. He becomes the Green Goblin after taking a serum that enhances his physical abilities but also drives him insane.
